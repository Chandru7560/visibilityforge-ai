# IncuBrix AEO, SEO, and GEO launch kit

**Status:** Proposed plan only. No campaign, publication, outreach, or external measurement described here has been executed.

## Initial audience hypothesis

Start with independent creators and small creator-led businesses looking for practical ways to plan, produce, publish, and grow content. Treat English-language users in India as the first test segment only if IncuBrix confirms that this audience and market fit its product, positioning, and customer data.

## Channels and rationale

1. **Owned site / SEO:** publish answer-first pages for real, source-verified creator questions; use technical recommendations only after an authorized audit.
2. **Answer engines / GEO:** measure the same question set manually or through an authorized provider, recording the exact query, date, location/profile, answer, and cited URLs.
3. **Creator communities and partner publications:** research relevant, real sources first; contribute useful material without implying a mention or link is guaranteed.
4. **Short-form video:** repurpose reviewed, evidence-backed answers into demos only after product claims and screenshots are approved.

No publisher, community, or competitor opportunity is claimed as researched. No external API or paid provider is configured in the project.

## First 30 days

### Week 1 — access and baseline design

- Confirm product positioning, ICP, markets, brand aliases, approved claims, owned URLs, and analytics access with IncuBrix.
- Build a source-linked question inventory; do not mark generated ideas as verified questions.
- Agree on measurement profiles, location, collection process, evidence retention, and audit authorization.
- Target: approve the measurement protocol and collect a first sample of 25 verified questions if access and sources permit.

### Weeks 2–4 — measurement and content experiments

- Complete a repeatable, evidence-backed baseline of up to 100 verified questions on two distinct dates, checking Google and at least four permitted AI assistants where feasible. Report actual coverage and failures; do not treat unavailable checks as negative results.
- Review authorized technical audit findings and validate each fix with the stated validator before calling it complete.
- Draft up to 10 pages from verified questions and checked sources; only label pages publish-ready after editorial and source review.
- Research up to 30 legitimate citation prospects; record relevance and competitor evidence only when directly observed.
- Compare repeat observations descriptively, with sample sizes and caveats.

These are proposed targets, not a forecast or achieved result. If scope, access, or provider limits prevent them, report the actual count and blocker.

## Measurement and reporting

- Baseline: question count, verified-source coverage, engine/profile coverage, distinct measurement dates, and failed/unavailable attempts.
- Content: reviewed drafts, source completeness, publication status, and page-level visits/conversions only when analytics access exists.
- Technical: observed issues, severity, evidence, validator, validation state, and recheck date.
- Citations: researched prospects and evidence quality; do not count prospects as acquired links.
- Report weekly from stored records; separate measured outcomes from targets and recommendations.

## Costs, access, and risks

- **Costs:** unknown until IncuBrix confirms approved tools, provider plans, and spend limits. No paid service or external AI/search API was used for this local project review.
- **Required access:** product demo/account, approved owned-site audit scope, CMS or publishing owner, analytics/Search Console if available, brand/style guidance, source and claim approvals, and authorized measurement accounts/providers.
- **Risks:** absent product access or source evidence; changing and personalized search/assistant outputs; rate limits and platform terms; unsupported product claims; crawler blind spots; publication review delays; and mistaking a prospect or draft for an achieved mention or published page.
- **Mitigations:** obtain written access/scope, preserve raw evidence and timestamps, record failures separately, use human review, validate changes, and label all targets/hypotheses clearly.
